How to play (the whole thing)
  • The deck: 54 cards, jokers included. A=1, 2–10 face, J=11, Q=12, K=13, joker=7.
  • The deal: everyone gets 3 cards, one card face-up on the field.
  • On your turn: make a set — hand cards + exactly one field card totalling 14, score the suit points of every card, draw back up to 4 and put one hand card on the field — or drop + draw one card. One set per turn.
  • Jokers: only ever pair joker-with-joker; never with 7s.
  • The end: when the deck runs out there's no drawing, and a set needs no after-discard. That includes the set that draws the last cards. Passing with nothing to play still puts a hand card on the field. A full round with no sets ends the game; cards left in hand score nothing.
  • Who starts: rock-paper-scissors first game, then lowest total points.
  • Computers: easy takes most sets sloppily & stacks randomly; medium takes the best set, cheapest-on-top; hard also stacks by lowest expected loss and keeps cards that are one field card from a set.
  • Mean mode: opponents' pile tops count as field cards. Steal one and its points move to you; you choose your stack order to bury valuable cards.

What does mean mode change?

The top card of each opponent's point pile also counts as a field card. Make a set with it and the card — and its points — move to your pile; the card underneath becomes their new top. You choose the order your set stacks, so you can bury good cards under a cheap top. Your own top is never usable by you. Computers steal too. With 4 players it's chaos mode.

    tips

    • count points, not cards. a set scores every card's suit: ♠ 4, ♥ 3, ♣ 2, ♦ 1. two spades beat four diamonds.
    • one field card, as many hand cards as it takes. the more of your hand goes into the set, the more it scores.
    • mind what you leave behind. after a set you put one hand card on the field. leave the one that is hardest to turn into 14.
    • jokers only go with jokers. a joker counts 7, but never pairs with a 7.
    • nothing to make? drop + draw. the card you drop goes to the field, so drop a cheap one.
    • mean mode: steal the tops. the top card of an opponent's pile counts as a field card. take it and its points move to you.
    • mean mode: bury the good ones. you choose how your set stacks. put your cheapest card on top.
    • the end comes fast. once the deck is gone there is no drawing, and cards left in your hand score nothing.
